步驟 4：利用距離關係列式

$$\frac{\sqrt6}{12}a - \frac{\sqrt6}{6} = 1$$
$$\frac{\sqrt6}{12}a = 1 + \frac{\sqrt6}{6} = \frac{6+\sqrt6}{6}$$

$$a = \frac{12}{\sqrt6} + 2 = 2\sqrt6 + 2$$
